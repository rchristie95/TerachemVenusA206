## What the range 21-26 cm^-1 actually is

It is a **functional** spread at matched basis, not a basis-convergence spread.
At def2-TZVPD both range-separated functionals are gauge-consistent to 0.5% and
bracket the moment at 7.84 (wB97X-D3) and 8.68 D (CAM-B3LYP). Since
J ~ |mu|^2 and the production value is 32.82 at 9.81 D:

    32.82 x (7.84/9.81)^2 = 21.0        32.82 x (8.68/9.81)^2 = 25.7

State it that way in the response. Note also that wB97X-D3 is still drifting
with basis (8.71 -> 8.24 -> 8.15 -> 7.84) whereas CAM-B3LYP is flat between
SVPD and TZVPD (8.69 -> 8.68).

## The caveat that must NOT be buried

TDDFT is ~130 nm too blue on this chromophore (401-406 nm against STEOM's 533
and experiment's 515). Because f = (2/3) dE |mu|^2, holding each TDDFT
oscillator strength and placing it at the STEOM energy returns
**CAM-B3LYP 9.95 D and wB97X-D3 9.04 D** -- i.e. back at STEOM's 9.81. So TDDFT
is not a clean replacement for the STEOM moment, and we should not present it
as one. This is the reason to quote a RANGE rather than a new point value.

## Why the tie still breaks against STEOM

The gauge ratio is f_vel/f_len = P2/(dE^2 D2), so it is not independent of the
excitation energy -- a method with a bad dE can fail it on that account alone.
That defence is available to TDDFT but **not** to STEOM:

| | ratio | its nm | nm required for gauge agreement |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| STEOM def2-SVP | 1.339 | 501.5 | **433** |
| STEOM def2-SVPD | 1.512 | 532.8 | **433** |

Both bases demand the *same* 433 nm, 82 nm away from the measured 515. STEOM's
excitation energy is the part it gets right, so it cannot buy gauge agreement
with an energy shift. The inconsistency sits in the moment (P2 against D2).

## Four independent handles, and STEOM is the outlier

| route | \|mu\| |
|---|---|
| extinction, eps_516 | 7.5 D (6.2-8.6 across width conventions) |
| Cusick's own published parameters, inverted | 7.6-7.9 D |
| wB97X-D3/def2-TZVPD, gauge 0.996 | 7.84 D |
| CAM-B3LYP/def2-TZVPD, gauge 0.995 | 8.68 D |
| **STEOM/def2-SVPD, gauge 1.512** | **9.81 D** |

Two experimental routes and two gauge-consistent electronic-structure routes
span 7.5-8.7. STEOM alone sits above them.

## What is NOT affected

TDC/PDA = 1.187 +/- 0.011 and the fractional multipole decomposition are
**dipole-independent** -- both TDC and PDA scale as |mu|^2, so the ratio cancels
it exactly. Reviewer 2's major point is about that ratio, so nothing here
reopens it. Only absolute cm^-1 values move.
